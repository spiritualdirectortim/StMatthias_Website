About Us

A Catholic parish in the Emerald Hills of Redwood City.

St. Matthias is a Roman Catholic community in the Archdiocese of San Francisco, home to about four hundred registered families and open to everyone seeking a faith community.

Our Parish & Mission

Rooted in Christ, open to everyone.

A small parish where people know one another, with room for anyone who is looking for a spiritual home.

St. Matthias Church is a Catholic Christian community in the Emerald Hills area of Redwood City and a parish of the Archdiocese of San Francisco. It has served this neighborhood since 1961, and today about four hundred registered families call it home.

Our mission

St. Matthias is a Roman Catholic community striving to respond to the call of Jesus through worship and sacraments, faith formation, service, and social opportunities.

Under the guidance of our pastor and pastoral staff, we work to strengthen the lay leaders who serve today and to develop and empower new ones. Although we are a small parish, we commit ourselves and our programs to hospitality, to generosity toward people in need, and to outreach to all who are seeking a faith community. As Catholics, we recognize the many challenges facing the Church, and we trust the Holy Spirit to guide us into the future.

How we live our mission

  • Worship and sacraments. Mass every weekend, Saturday at 5:00 p.m. and Sunday at 9:45 a.m., with the Sunday Mass livestreamed, and preparation for all seven sacraments. Explore the sacraments
  • Faith formation. Adult, intergenerational, and family-based programs for seekers at every stage of faith, from GIFT (Generations in Faith Together) for families to Becoming Catholic for adults. See faith formation
  • Service. Parishioners who respond to neighbors’ urgent needs through the St. Vincent de Paul Society, bring Communion to the sick and homebound, and walk with grieving families. Find a ministry
  • Social opportunities. Monthly Welcome Weekends and parish gatherings where newcomers and longtime parishioners get to know one another. See upcoming events
  • St. Matthias Preschool. A full-day, year-round Catholic preschool that is a ministry of the parish. Visit the preschool website (opens in a new tab)

Pastoral Council

The Pastoral Council works to deepen the Christian commitment of parishioners and to help the parish grow. Its members study major decisions and priorities and make recommendations to the pastor and parish staff. Council members also serve as Welcome Ambassadors, reaching out to new parish and preschool families by phone or email.

Finance Council

Church law requires every parish to have a Finance Council to help guide its financial and temporal matters. The council is advisory: its members, who bring experience in business, finance, law, or accounting, advise the pastor and staff on budgeting, financial statements, and financial decisions. Members are active parishioners who are not employed by the parish or preschool, and they normally serve three-year terms.

New Here? FAQ

Questions newcomers often ask.

Don’t see your question? Call (650) 366-9544 or email info@stmatthiasparish.org. We’re glad to help.

When and where is Mass?

Mass is celebrated every Saturday at 5:00 p.m. and every Sunday at 9:45 a.m. at St. Matthias Church, 1685 Cordilleras Road, Redwood City, CA 94062. Come as you are and sit wherever you like.

Get directions (opens in a new tab)

Can I watch Mass online before I visit?

Yes. The Sunday 9:45 a.m. Mass is livestreamed on the parish YouTube channel, so you can get a feel for the parish from home.

Watch Mass online (opens YouTube in a new tab)

What is Welcome Weekend?

On the second full weekend of each month, St. Matthias holds Welcome Weekend at both Masses. People attending for the first time and visiting parishioners are welcomed by the parish and receive a special blessing.

After the Saturday 5:00 p.m. Mass, appetizers are served and a movie about a saint is shown; you are invited to bring a dish to share. After the Sunday 9:45 a.m. Mass, there are donuts, coffee, and juice in Fr. Lacey Hall.

How do I register with the parish?

Registering helps the parish staff know who you are and better meet your needs, and you will receive news from the parish about upcoming events.

Register with the parish (opens in a new tab)

I’m interested in becoming Catholic. Where do I start?

Welcome. St. Matthias walks with adults who are exploring the Catholic faith, including people who were baptized in another Christian church and Catholics who have not yet received Confirmation or First Communion. Call the parish office to talk with Deacon Rich Foley or Deacon David Rolandelli; there is no obligation.

Learn about Becoming Catholic

How do I arrange a baptism, wedding, or another sacrament?

Each sacrament has its own preparation. For a baptism, call the parish office to set up an informational appointment with Deacon Rich Foley. For a wedding, contact the parish at least six months before your proposed date.

See how to prepare for each sacrament

What is there for children and teens?

Families with children in kindergarten through eighth grade can join GIFT (Generations in Faith Together), the parish’s family-based faith formation program, which is also the path to First Communion. Middle and high school students have youth ministry, and high school students can prepare for Confirmation. When volunteers are available, young children ages 3 to 6 can take part in Promise, a Children’s Liturgy of the Word during the 9:45 a.m. Sunday Mass.

See children and family programs

When can I go to confession?

The Sacrament of Reconciliation is available by appointment. Call the parish office at (650) 366-9544 to arrange a time.

Someone I love is sick or homebound. Can the parish help?

Yes. Call the parish office to arrange for a parishioner to bring Holy Communion, for a visit from a priest or deacon, or for the Anointing of the Sick.

About the Anointing of the Sick

How can I get involved?

There are ways to serve at Mass, care for neighbors, welcome newcomers, and help with the work of the parish. Training is provided for every ministry.
