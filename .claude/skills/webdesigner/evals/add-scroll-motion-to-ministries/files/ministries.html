Find your place to serve.

Every ministry below is open to newcomers. Pick one that fits your gifts and your week, and the leader will be in touch.

  • Choir

    Thursdays 7pm. Readers of music and non-readers both welcome.

  • Food pantry

    Saturdays 9am to noon. Sorting, packing and greeting.

  • Lectors

    Proclaim the readings at Sunday Mass. Training provided.

  • Youth group

    Grades 6 to 12. Sunday evenings after the 5pm Mass.

  • Hospitality

    Greet, usher and host coffee after Mass once a month.

  • St. Vincent de Paul

    Home visits and practical help for neighbors in need.
