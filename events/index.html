Masses of Remembrance

When
November 7–8 · Saturday 5:00 p.m. · Sunday 9:45 a.m.
Where
St. Matthias Church

Caroling, Cookies & Cocoa

Saturday, December 12 · 2:30–5:00 p.m.

Every week and every month

Regular gatherings.

Welcome

Welcome Weekend

When
Second full weekend of each month · After both weekend Masses
Where
St. Matthias Church and Fr. Lacey Hall

New to St. Matthias? Receive a special blessing at Mass, then stay: appetizers and a movie about a saint after Saturday Mass, donuts and coffee in Fr. Lacey Hall after Sunday Mass.

Prayer

Rosary for Peace

When
Thursdays · 10:00 a.m.
Where
St. Matthias Church

Pray the Rosary together in the church for peace in our world. Never prayed it before? Just follow along.

Every weekend

Mass at St. Matthias.

Mass is celebrated every Saturday at 5:00 p.m. and every Sunday at 9:45 a.m. at St. Matthias Church, 1685 Cordilleras Road, Redwood City. The Sunday Mass is also livestreamed.

Questions about an event? Call the parish office at (650) 366-9544 or email info@stmatthiasparish.org.
