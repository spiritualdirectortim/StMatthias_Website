Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated November 4, 2026.

Summary

A simple website that collects very little.

This website shares information about St. Matthias Catholic Church. It has no accounts, no forms, and no advertising.

Who we are

This website is operated by St. Matthias Catholic Church, 1685 Cordilleras Road, Redwood City, CA 94062, a Roman Catholic parish of the Archdiocese of San Francisco.

Information this website collects

You can read every page of this website without giving us any personal information. The website does not ask you to create an account or fill out a form, and it does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Like most websites, the service that hosts these pages may automatically record basic technical information when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page you visited, to keep the site secure and running reliably.

To display text in the site’s typefaces, pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when the fonts are requested. Google’s privacy policy applies to that service.

Outside services we link to

Some parish activities happen on other websites. When you follow these links, you leave this website, and that service’s own privacy policy applies to any information you share there:

  • Online giving: gifts are made through Vanco, the parish’s secure giving provider.
  • Parish registration and email updates: Flocknote.
  • Livestreamed Mass: YouTube.
  • Directions: Google Maps.
  • Social media: Facebook and Instagram.
  • St. Matthias Preschool: the preschool’s own website.

Contacting the parish

If you email or call the parish office, your message is received by parish staff so that they can respond to you.

Children

This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way this website handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Questions

Contact the parish office at (650) 366-9544 or info@stmatthiasparish.org.
