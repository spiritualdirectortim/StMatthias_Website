Where to start

The gifts that keep our doors open.

Not sure where to give? Start here. Every gift is processed securely by Vanco, the parish’s online giving provider.

Primary way to give

Regular Collection

Gifts to the Regular Collection are the primary source of funds for the parish. They meet St. Matthias’s operating and program expenses: keeping the church open for Mass and the sacraments, supporting faith formation for children, youth, and adults, and sustaining the ministries that welcome newcomers and care for neighbors.

You can give once or set up a recurring gift, and an email receipt is sent for each gift.

Give to the Regular Collection

Active parish campaign

St. Matthias Roofing Fund

One of the biggest maintenance projects for any church is replacing the roof that keeps the building sound. The Parish and Finance Councils of St. Matthias anticipate the need to replace the church’s composite roof in the next three to five years.

Gifts to the Roofing Fund are set aside for that project.

Give to the Roofing Fund

Which fund should I give to?

If you are not sure, choose the Regular Collection. It is the primary source of funds for the parish and covers the everyday costs of worship, faith formation, and ministry.

Can I give some other way than online?

Yes. Call the parish office at (650) 366-9544 or email info@stmatthiasparish.org, and we’ll help you find the way that works for you.

Do I pay Faith Formation fees here?

Yes. Annual fees for GIFT, OCIA for Youth, Middle School Youth Ministry, First Communion, and Confirmation preparation are paid through Faith Formation. Paying the fee completes your registration.

What is a Mass stipend?

When a Mass is offered for a special intention or a loved one who has died, a customary Mass stipend of $10 is given.
