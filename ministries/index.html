Ministries

Bring your gifts. Serve as Jesus did.

Every baptized Christian is called to serve and has gifts to share. Pray about where you are being called, then reach out: training is provided for every ministry at St. Matthias.

Worship at Mass Serve Our Neighbors Welcome & Community Care & Support Help the Parish

Worship at Mass

Help the whole parish pray.

Lay ministers have served the liturgy for centuries as musicians and servers, and today also as readers and ministers of Holy Communion.

Music Ministry

All are welcome to sing or play an instrument. No experience is needed, and you don’t need to read music; you can learn at the Sunday Morning Music Fellowship. The music ministry rehearses one hour before each Mass: Saturday at 4:00 p.m. and Sunday at 8:45 a.m.

Contact: Melanie Dresbach, Music Director

Sunday Morning Music Fellowship

Join Melanie for coffee, treats, fellowship, and singing at 8:15 a.m. in the hall before Sunday Mass. You don’t have to join the music ministry; just come for the joy of praying through song.

Contact: Melanie Dresbach

Readers

Readers proclaim the Word of God at Mass and other liturgies and also read the prayers of the faithful and parish announcements. Confirmed adults and youth are invited, and readers are scheduled in rotation at the Mass they usually attend.

Training and contact: Deacon David Rolandelli

Eucharistic Ministers

Extraordinary ministers of Holy Communion help distribute the Body and Blood of Christ at Mass. Any Catholic who has received Baptism, Confirmation, and the Eucharist and is willing to make a commitment may serve. Training is provided.

Contact: the parish office

Altar Servers

Young people who have received First Communion are encouraged to deepen their participation in the liturgy by serving with the priest and deacon at Mass. Training is provided.

Contact: Deacon Rich Foley

Ushers

Ushers help people find seats, organize the collection and the offertory, and are ready to help if an emergency arises during Mass.

Contact: the parish office

Prayer & Worship Commission

Members meet once a month to evaluate and plan parish liturgies, working to make every part of our worship as meaningful as possible and helping to beautify the worship space.

Contact: Deacon Rich Foley

Rosary for Peace

A group of parishioners gathers weekly in the church to pray the Rosary for peace in our world. Everyone is welcome to join.

See when it meets

Serve Our Neighbors

Care for people in need, close to home.

Putting Catholic Social Teaching into practice in Redwood City and beyond.

St. Vincent de Paul Society

The parish conference lives out Jesus’ command to care for the poor by responding to emergency needs for food, shelter, clothing, and furniture, and by visiting people who are sick or in jail. Parishioners bring food donations to Sunday Masses, and special collections help meet urgent needs.

Contact: the parish office

Justice and Charity Commission

The commission helps parishioners learn about and live out Catholic Social Teaching, as individuals and as a community. Members plan service opportunities and share information about local, national, and global justice issues.

Contact: the parish office

Maple Street Shelter Dinner

Each year, the Justice and Charity Commission organizes a dinner for the residents of the Maple Street Shelter in Redwood City. Parishioners cook and donate dishes; middle and high school youth set up, serve, share the meal, and lead a game of bingo; and parents help as chaperones and drivers.

Contact: the parish office

Welcome & Community

Make every newcomer feel at home.

Hospitality is one of the commitments at the heart of our mission.

Welcome Weekend Hospitality

Each month, volunteers organize the greetings, hospitality, and refreshments offered to everyone who comes to Mass on Welcome Weekend, from Saturday appetizers to Sunday donuts and coffee.

About Welcome Weekend

Greeters

Greeters welcome parishioners and guests as they arrive at church, help newcomers find their way, and point anyone with questions to the parish office. After Mass, they thank everyone who worshiped with us.

Contact: the parish office

Welcome Ambassadors

Members of the Pastoral Council reach out by phone or email to welcome new parish and preschool families and introduce them to parish programs and services.

About the Pastoral Council

Care & Support

Walk with people through illness, loss, and joy.

If you or someone you love needs care, call the parish office at (650) 366-9544.

Supporting the Sick and Homebound

When a parishioner can’t drive to church, this ministry connects them with someone willing to offer a ride. When a parishioner is homebound, others visit and bring Communion, keeping them connected to the parish community.

Contact: the parish office

Ministers to the Sick and Homebound

Trained Eucharistic ministers bring Holy Communion to people in Sequoia and Kaiser hospitals, care and rehabilitation facilities, and homes in and around Redwood City.

Anointing of the Sick

Funeral Support Team

Team members support families as they plan a funeral and through the celebration of their loved one’s life and the reception that follows.

Contact: the parish office

Prayer Shawl Ministry

A ministry of prayer and of the hands. Members make prayer shawls, scarves, and quilts for people celebrating births and weddings or facing illness and loss, praying for each person as they work. The group meets on the third Tuesday of the month at 3:00 p.m. in Fr. Lacey Hall.

Contact: the parish office

Prayer Tree Network

Anyone may ask for prayer for a special need or intention, such as healing, grief, loss, discernment, strength, or comfort. Each member of the network prays for these intentions, and every request and contact detail is kept strictly confidential.

To share a request: the parish office

Help the Parish

Share your time and skills behind the scenes.

Christian stewardship means receiving God’s gifts gratefully and sharing them generously.

Collection Committee

Rotating groups of parishioners count the weekly collections, then record and deposit donations according to Archdiocesan guidelines.

Contact: the parish office

Office Assistance

Occasional projects in the parish or preschool office need many hands. Most involve folding, collating, or labeling, and you can help when it suits you.

Contact: the parish office

Facility Support and Operations

Parishioners with carpentry, plumbing, electrical, or other skills help with maintenance and minor repairs as needed, saving the parish time and resources.

Contact: the parish office

Parish councils and giving

The Pastoral and Finance Councils advise the pastor on parish priorities and finances, and financial gifts sustain everything described on this page.

Support St. Matthias

Ready to get involved? Call (650) 366-9544 or email info@stmatthiasparish.org, and the parish office will connect you with the right person.
